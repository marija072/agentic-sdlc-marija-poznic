# arc42 Overview

This document summarizes the purpose of each of the 12 arc42 architecture documentation chapters.

## Chapter Descriptions

1. **Introduction and Goals:** Describes the purpose of the system, its main goals, and stakeholders.
2. **Constraints:** Defines the technical, organizational, and other limitations that influence the architecture.
3. **Context and Scope:** Defines the system boundaries and its interactions with users and external systems.
4. **Solution Strategy:** Explains the overall architectural approach and the main technology choices.
5. **Building Block View:** Describes the system's main components and how they are organized.
6. **Runtime View:** Explains how system components interact during important operations.
7. **Deployment View:** Describes the infrastructure and environments where the system runs.
8. **Cross-cutting Concepts:** Documents common approaches to concerns such as error handling, logging, and security.
9. **Architecture Decisions:** Records important architectural decisions and the reasons behind them.
10. **Quality Requirements:** Defines the quality attributes and measurable expectations for the system.
11. **Risks and Technical Debts:** Identifies potential risks, known weaknesses, and possible mitigation strategies.
12. **Glossary:** Defines important terms used throughout the architecture documentation.
